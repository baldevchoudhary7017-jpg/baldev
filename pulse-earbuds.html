Engineering

Built for signal clarity

Every part of PULSE Air is tuned around one idea: less of what you don't want to hear, more of what you do.

Adaptive ANC — 42dB

Six microphones sample the room 400 times a second and cancel it out before it reaches your ear.

30 hours, case included

8 hours per charge, 4 more full charges in the case. A 10-minute top-up buys 2 hours of playback.

Spatial audio

Head-tracked sound that stays anchored to the screen, not your ears, for film and games alike.

Touch controls

A single strip on each stem handles playback, calls and ANC — mapped how you like, in the app.

IPX5 water resistant

Rain, sweat and gym humidity are all fine. Rinse under a tap if you need to — just skip the pool.

Wireless charging

Drop the case on any Qi pad. No cable, no fumbling for the right port at 1am.
